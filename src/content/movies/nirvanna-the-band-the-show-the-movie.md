I've now watched this enough times that the diary entries above stopped being
reviews and started being one-word bits. So here's the actual writeup.

The thing I keep coming back to is how much of it must have been real. Not
"based on a true story" real — actually-filmed-in-public, nobody-signed-a-release
real. Every time the movie cuts to a wide shot you find yourself scanning the
background for someone who clearly did not know they were in a film.

Watch it blind. Then watch the webseries. Then lose a month.
