Growing up is a lot like losing your religion. I don't believe in God anymore, and I don't believe in my father either.

I miss the home I used to go home to. I miss going back to my family where I was the number one priority and all attention went to me when I'm there. Now its a fractured home.

I am still clinging onto the home I used to experience when I would home home from school as a child. Now everytime I go back it's not how it was, and never will be.

But why do I still cling onto hope?

I don't think I miss the home, but the idea of home when I was a child.
