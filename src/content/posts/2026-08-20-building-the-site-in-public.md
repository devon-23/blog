Quick rundown of how this thing actually works, for anyone curious:

- Every article, think piece, and recommendation is a plain text file with a date on it. Adding new content means adding a new file — nothing ever gets edited away or removed.
- Every month automatically gets its own recap page, built from whatever was added that month. August 2026 is the first one.
- Each month also gets a small set of randomly drawn goals — things like "finish a book" or "bake a cake" — checked off as they happen.
- The Music window pulls live stats and now-playing straight from Last.fm.

Everything else is just window dressing — literally, in this case.
