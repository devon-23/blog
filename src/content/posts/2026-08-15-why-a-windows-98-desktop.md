---
title: Why a Windows 98 desktop
type: thinkpiece
date: 2026-08-15
summary: Some notes on why this site looks like it's running Windows 98, and why that fits better than a normal blog layout.
tags: [design, nostalgia]
---

A normal blog is a scroll. Post, post, post, newest on top, everything else buried under an archive link nobody clicks. That never felt like the right shape for this.

What I actually wanted was something closer to a desktop — a place with distinct rooms for distinct things. Music lives in one window. Recommendations live in another. Longer pieces get opened like documents. And the whole thing keeps a literal history, month by month, the way a computer keeps files instead of quietly overwriting itself.

Windows 98 is the version of a "computer" most legible to basically everyone at a glance — a taskbar, a Start button, windows you can drag around — while still being distinct enough to actually feel like a place, not a template. That's the idea, anyway. More windows to come.
