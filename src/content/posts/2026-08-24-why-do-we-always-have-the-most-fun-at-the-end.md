Anticipated nostalgia might be one of the most bittersweet feelings of all time. I know I'm living the exact experience I want, but I'm already mourning the end and the grief of the moment ending.

Everything seems funnier, smiles are wider, and lite seems brighter.
But why didn't it feel that way the entire time, and why must it only feel serene at the end?

Knowing it's all about to come to an end and knowing you're already in the good old days is tough because you're grateful for the experience but sad it's coming to an end.
