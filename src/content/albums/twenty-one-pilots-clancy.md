My most-played record, which the scrobble count is not subtle about.

Last.fm can tell you I played this 371 times. It can't tell you that most of
those were the same three songs on the same walk, or that the back half of the
album took me about a year to actually hear properly. That's what this page is
for — the number is the easy part.
